# About Us

Dambel puts gyms, trainers and athletes in one app. A gym owner runs their membership plans and their subscribers; a trainer writes workout and diet plans and sells training services; everybody else trains, logs what they did, and watches it add up.

---

## What Dambel does

Four things, and they connect to each other:

- **Gyms.** Find one near you on a map, look at its hours, equipment and plans, and subscribe. If you run a gym, you list it, price its plans and manage the people on them — see [Gyms](gyms.md).
- **Training.** Find a trainer, buy a service, and get the plans they write for you. If you train people, you publish services and write those plans — see [Trainers](trainers.md).
- **Plans.** Workout plans down to the set, and diet plans down to the meal, either written by hand or drafted with the [AI assistant](ai-assistant.md).
- **Tracker.** What you actually did — workouts, meals, water, sleep, weight — with averages, charts and personal records on top of it. See [Tracker](tracker.md).

## Who it is for

Dambel carries three kinds of account, and one person can be all three at once:

| You are | What Dambel gives you |
|---|---|
| Training for yourself | Plans to follow, a tracker to fill in, and gyms and trainers to find |
| A trainer | Trainees, services to sell, and plans to write for the people who buy them |
| A gym owner | A listing, membership plans, subscribers, check-ins and the money side |

Nothing is switched on by role name. What you can do follows from what you have — upload an approved trainer licence and the training tools appear; list a gym and the gym tools appear.

## The company

Dambel is built by **Parsa Smart Sports Innovators** — فناوران ورزش هوشمند پارسا — which has been operating since April 2024. The aim has not changed since: make the ordinary parts of training, coaching and running a gym take less effort than they used to, and keep the data that comes out of them in one place the person it belongs to controls.

## Getting in touch

Support is by email, at **support@dambel.io**. [Contact Us](contact.md) says what to send; the [FAQ](faq.md) answers most of what people write in about; and [Reporting](reporting.md) is the route for a gym, trainer or user who is doing something they should not.
