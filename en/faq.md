# FAQ

The questions support gets most often, with the page that answers each one in full.

---

## Getting started

### What is Dambel?

One app for gyms, trainers and athletes. Find a gym or a trainer, follow the workout and diet plans written for you, and log what you actually do. [What is Dambel?](getting-started.md) is the tour.

### Do I have to pay to use Dambel?

No. Finding gyms and trainers, following plans and logging your tracker are all free. Money only comes into it when you buy something — a gym membership, a training service, Premium, or a Boost on your own listing. [Premium, Boost and money](monetization.md) covers all four.

### I forgot my password. What now?

Use **Forgot password?** on the login screen. Dambel sends a code to your phone number, you enter it, and you set a new password. [Authentication Guide](authentication.md) walks through it.

## Gyms and trainers

### How do I find a gym or a trainer?

Gyms are in the Gyms section, as a list or on a map you can pan — see [Gyms](gyms.md). Trainers are in the Training section, where you can search and open anybody's profile — see [Trainers](trainers.md).

### My trainer licence still says awaiting review. What is taking so long?

A person checks each document, so it is not instant. You get a notification the moment it is approved or rejected, and if it is rejected the reason is shown right under the status so you know what to replace. [Profile](profile.md) lists the four states.

### What does Boost actually do?

It promotes one listing — a gym or a training service — for a number of days you choose. While it runs, the listing sorts to the top of results and carries a Boost badge; on a gym the map pin is coloured for it too. When the days run out, nothing else changes. See [Premium, Boost and money](monetization.md).

## Money

### How do I put money in my wallet?

**Request deposit** in the [Wallet](wallet.md) opens the amount box and then your bank's payment page.

### I paid, but my balance has not changed.

A deposit is only credited once the gateway confirms it, which is not instant. Until then the wallet shows a banner with the amount, a **Check status** button and a way back to the payment page — so a payment you completed is visible rather than lost. If it still has not landed after that, write to support@dambel.io.

### Why was I charged a commission for adding my own trainee?

Because that person paid you outside the app, so Dambel collected nothing from them — the commission for the service or plan comes out of your wallet instead. The amount is always shown before you confirm, and you can back out there. A purchase made inside the app works the ordinary way and costs you no commission. See [Premium, Boost and money](monetization.md).

## Your data

### Can my trainer see my tracker?

Only if you share it with them. Nobody sees your tracker otherwise. You create the share yourself, choose who and between which dates, and deleting it takes their access away immediately. A shared tracker is read-only — they can look, not edit. See [Tracker](tracker.md).

### What does the AI assistant know about me?

The screen you are on, and the data you could open yourself. It acts with your access, so it can read a tracker shared with you and cannot reach one that is not. See [AI Assistant](ai-assistant.md) and the [Privacy Policy](privacy.md).

### How do I delete my account?

You cannot yet — there is no control in the app and no process behind one. That is a gap we have not closed, and the [Privacy Policy](privacy.md) says so plainly rather than implying otherwise. Questions about your data go to support@dambel.io.

## Everything else

### Can I use Dambel on a computer?

Yes, in a browser, and you can install it as an app from Chrome. [Using Dambel on a computer](on-a-computer.md) covers what changes on a wide screen.

### Somebody is misusing Dambel. How do I report them?

Report the gym, trainer, user or plan from its own screen, so the review team gets the record along with your report. [Reporting](reporting.md) explains what happens next.
